What We Build

Remodeling and finish work,
done to a higher tolerance.

We take on the interior work where the details are the whole job — where a joint that's a sixteenth off is the first thing you'd see every morning. Most projects run from a single room to a full interior renovation.

01

Kitchen Remodeling

Layout changes, cabinetry installation, countertops, backsplash tile, lighting, and the trim and scribe work that makes cabinets look built into the house instead of set against it.

02

Bathroom Remodeling

Tiled showers built on properly waterproofed assemblies, custom niches and benches, vanities, and fixtures set plumb and true. Done right behind the tile, not just on it.

03

Custom Trim & Millwork

Crown moulding, casing and baseboard, wainscoting and panelled walls, coffered ceilings, built-ins, shelving, and stair details. Mitres tight, returns clean, no caulk hiding the cuts.

04

Tile & Stone

Large-format porcelain, mosaic, herringbone and chevron layouts, and natural stone. Substrates flattened first so the finished surface is flat, the lippage is nil, and the grout lines run straight.

05

Basement Finishing

Framing, electrical and wiring, insulation, drywall, flooring, and finish carpentry — built and detailed so the finished space reads like the rest of your home, not like a basement.

06

Whole-Home Interior Renovation

Multi-room and full-floor renovations, sequenced and staged so your household keeps functioning while the work moves through it. One point of contact from demo to punch list.

Also available: interior painting · drywall repair · doors and hardware · flooring installation · electrical and wiring repair · trim and moulding · furniture assembly · picture hanging and art installation · general home improvements and handyman work.

Why Linkpoint Elen

Four reasons homeowners
stop shopping and hire us.

Remodeling has a reputation problem, and it's earned. Here's specifically how we work differently — and how you can hold us to it.

One craftsman, start to finish

You won't meet a salesman, then get handed to a rotating crew of subs. Vitalii walks your job, prices it, and does the work himself. The standard doesn't change between the estimate and the punch list, because the person doesn't.

Finish-level standards

Square, level, plumb, and tight — checked, not eyeballed. We spend the extra hours on substrate prep, layout, and the transitions most people only ever notice when they're wrong. That's the whole difference between a remodel and a high-end remodel.

Clean sites, respected homes

Floors and pathways protected, dust contained at the source, tools and debris packed out at the end of every day. You still live here while we work, and the site gets treated that way.

Straight answers on price and timeline

A written scope before anything gets demoed, so you know what's included and what isn't. If something changes once the walls are open — and on older homes it sometimes does — you hear it from us that day, with the cost, before we proceed.

About Us

A trade learned where
the details weren't optional.

Linkpoint Elen Construction was founded by Vitalii Korostii, who learned finish carpentry and interior work in Ukraine — a trade culture where a craftsman's reputation travels by word of mouth and nothing gets covered up with caulk and a hope that nobody looks.

He brought that standard to Ohio and built the business here the same way: one job at a time, on referrals from people who were happy enough to hand out his name. Linkpoint Elen stays deliberately small so that every project gets the owner's own hands and full attention rather than whichever crew happened to be free that week.

The work is intentionally focused on high-end interiors — kitchens, baths, custom trim and millwork, tile and stone — because that's where craftsmanship is actually visible, and where the difference between good and exceptional is something you notice every single day you live in the room.

Today Linkpoint Elen serves homeowners throughout Loveland, Mason, Montgomery, Madeira, Symmes Township, Blue Ash, Indian Hill, Milford, and the surrounding Cincinnati suburbs.
